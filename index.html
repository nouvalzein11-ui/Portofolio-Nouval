Automation · Product matching

Product Finder: hours of catalog searching, done in seconds

Sales teams receive lists of customer requests: where the customer is travelling, how much internet data they need, for how many days, and sometimes a preferred vendor. Product Finder checks every request against a catalog of 30,000+ products, applies the business rules, and returns a file that is ready to quote. What used to be hours of manual searching now takes seconds.

  • 2 product catalogsDigital SIMs (eSIM) and physical SIM cards, each stored once
  • 18 details per requestMatched product code, price, ID check, validity, renewal, vendor and status
  • Paste or uploadType requests on the page or upload a list; download the result as Excel or CSV
